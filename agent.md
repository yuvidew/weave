# Daily OpenAI & Anthropic Research Agent

## Objective

Create a daily research agent that monitors the web for the latest and most useful developments related to **OpenAI / ChatGPT** and **Anthropic / Claude**.

The agent should find meaningful new articles, research, product announcements, model updates, safety developments, and other important industry news, then turn them into a concise but useful research report.

## Daily Workflow

Every day at **10:00 AM IST**:

1. Search the web for newly published and updated information about:

   * OpenAI
   * ChatGPT
   * GPT models
   * OpenAI research and products
   * Anthropic
   * Claude
   * Anthropic research and products
   * AI agents and agent-related developments from OpenAI or Anthropic

2. Prioritize information published since the previous daily report.

3. Prefer high-quality and authoritative sources, especially:

   * Official OpenAI sources
   * Official Anthropic sources
   * Research papers
   * Reputable technology publications
   * Major news organizations

4. Avoid:

   * Duplicate articles
   * Low-quality content
   * SEO spam
   * Rumors without credible sourcing
   * Minor updates that have little practical value

## Research Summary

For each important story, include:

### 1. Title

Clear title of the development.

### 2. Source

Publication/source name and original article link.

### 3. Published Date

When the article or announcement was published.

### 4. Summary

A concise explanation of what happened.

### 5. Why It Matters

Explain why the development is important for AI developers, users, businesses, or the broader AI industry.

### 6. Key Takeaways

List the most important points.

### 7. Practical Impact

Explain what developers or AI users should consider doing differently, if applicable.

### 8. Sources

Include links to the original sources so the information can be verified.

## Report Structure

Start the report with:

**Daily AI Research Brief — [Date]**

Then include:

* **Executive Summary**
* **Top OpenAI / ChatGPT Developments**
* **Top Anthropic / Claude Developments**
* **Research & Technical Developments**
* **AI Agents & Developer Updates**
* **Important Industry Signals**
* **What to Watch Next**
* **Sources**

Only include the most useful developments. Quality is more important than quantity.
